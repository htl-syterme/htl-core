3. The Service

X-Trust is a server-side verification layer. Your backend sends a request to our endpoint with the signals it can legitimately observe about the session. The Service evaluates those signals and, when the configured threshold is met, returns a signed HTTP header proving that a human was present in the loop. The header is signed with HMAC-SHA256 over a canonical string of the response fields, using a shared secret assigned to your API key. You validate the signature on your own infrastructure.

What the Service is: a signal you can use to reduce automated abuse, scripted traffic and bot-driven actions in your own product. What the Service is not: an identity, age, KYC, AML, sanctions or background verification service. It does not tell you who a person is.
